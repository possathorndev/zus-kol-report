## Source and calculation notes

- Full source exports remain unchanged in archieve/sb and archieve/yonny (superwadee). The input CSVs contain only rows with numeric platform views. Their NO. values identify the original archive row numbers.
- SB has 31 KOLs with TikTok metrics and 24 with Instagram Reels metrics. All 31 have source status Done. Candidate and cancelled rows without metrics are excluded.
- Yonny has 10 KOLs in Platinum Pop 0726 and 3 in Seacon Square 0726. The Seacon Square rows retain source status In Process and are included because they have metrics. They should not be described as confirmed completed work.
- The SB source summary omits Instagram reposts. The report includes 32 reposts under the shared engagement formula, bringing total engagements to 5,386.
- Yonny has no reach columns. Summary boxes labelled Reach are not used as reach measurements. The report hides reach and the summary CSV leaves it blank. Calculations use the detailed platform columns.
- Engagements equal likes plus comments plus shares plus reposts plus saves. TikTok reposts are unavailable and count as zero under the existing calculation.
- The HTML displays mean KOL ER. The CSV includes that measure and aggregate ER, which divides total engagements by total views.
- Missing engagement components count as zero under the existing generator. Reach totals sum available post reach and do not deduplicate viewers.
- Facebook and other channels are outside the generator metric schema and are not included.
