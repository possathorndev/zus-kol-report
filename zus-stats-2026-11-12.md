## Calculation notes

- The report includes only the 19 rows marked Done. Candidate and cancelled rows remain in the source snapshot, with their original sheet row numbers.
- Portobello has 5 TikTok posts and 4 Instagram Reels. AIA Connect has 5 of each. Village Hub has 9 TikTok posts and 8 Instagram Reels.
- Portobello row 13, yakjapaii, has Lemon8 links and a note that Lemon8 replaces Instagram. Its 106 views and 1 like are excluded from Instagram totals. They remain in the source snapshot. Including Lemon8 separately brings all-platform views to 41,669 and engagements to 1,697.
- Engagements equal likes plus comments plus shares plus reposts plus saves. TikTok has no repost metric in this source.
- Aggregate ER is total engagements divided by total views, multiplied by 100. The HTML report displays mean KOL ER, which averages individual post rates. The CSV includes both.
- Missing metrics are unavailable, not verified zeros. The existing generator uses zero for missing engagement components. Its Instagram tables do not distinguish those missing components from reported zero.
- Reach totals sum reported post reach. They do not measure unique people across posts, platforms, or campaigns.
- These are the metrics recorded in the sheet at retrieval time. Live TikTok, Instagram, and Lemon8 counts were not checked.
- The previous data files are preserved in archieve/zus-102026. The Google Sheet was read only. Molly Tea and Sermit Tower are outside this report.
